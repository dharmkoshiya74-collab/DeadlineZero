## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated intensity of high-output academic research, engineered through the lens of elite developer workspaces. It bridges scholarly rigor with modern toolchain velocity—transforming chaotic thesis drafting, peer-review cycles, lab datasets, and impending submission deadlines into an operational command center.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Editorial** and **Technical Utilitarianism**:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian Surface Layers:** Deep, calculated voids replace soft consumer interfaces. Visual hierarchy is established through hairline boundaries and nuanced tonal luminosity rather than heavy blurs or artificial skeuomorphism.
- **Micro-Metric Telemetry:** Research artifacts, computational runtimes, and citations are treated like production deployments. Metadata is dense, scannable, and monospaced where mechanical precision is required.
- **Zero-Latency Visual Feedback:** Interactions feel instantaneous and definitive. Focus states, command palettes, and pipeline statuses cut through low-luminance surfaces with laser-calibrated chromatic accents.
- **Emotional Tone:** Disciplined, hyper-competent, calm under pressure, and unapologetically engineered for intellectual heavy-lifting.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated luminescence over an obsidian baseline. High saturation is reserved exclusively for operational states, AI synthesis, and critical deadline vectors.

### Surface Architecture & Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#090A0F`):** Deepest void ground for foundational viewports and root shells.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0F1117`):** Elevated structural containers, persistent navigation rails, and sidebars.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#161922`):** Interactive cards, code execution panes, and document workspace modules.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#1F2430`):** Floating dropdowns, hover elevations, and active modal overlays.
- **Hairline Border Soft (`#232735`):** Structural partition lines and low-emphasis grid divides.
- **Hairline Border Strong (`#2E3446`):** Focused card perimeters, input boundaries, and key divider thresholds.

### Typographic Contrast Hierarchy
- **Text Primary (`#F3F4F6`):** High-clarity editorial text, main headlines, and active numeric readouts.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Paragraphs, metadata values, abstract previews, and secondary labels.
- **Text Tertiary (`#64748B`):** Keyboard shortcuts, timestamps, disabled triggers, and schema keys.

### Chromatic Signal System
- **Electric Lime (`#00F59B` / `#10B981`):** Represents AI synthesis, dynamic pipeline progress, verified citations, and positive submission readiness.
- **Deep Cobalt / Indigo (`#6366F1` / `#3B82F6`):** Primary interaction hooks, focused deep-work sessions, active cursor sync, and repository branch controls.
- **Scholastic Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Approaching review windows, preprint embargo warnings, and pending mentor approvals.
- **Urgent Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Imminent submission cutoff (<24h), broken bibliography links, and compiler/pipeline errors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples the razor-sharp, geometric neutrality of `Geist` for rapid information intake with `JetBrains Mono` for structured academic telemetry, LaTeX expressions, bibtex keys, and countdown timers.

### Usage Directives
- **Hierarchical Restraint:** Maintain a tight baseline grid. Avoid jumpy typographic hierarchies; let weight differences (`500` vs `400`) and value transitions (`#F3F4F6` to `#94A3B8`) designate structural priority.
- **Monospace Deployment:** `JetBrains Mono` is reserved exclusively for:
  - Time-to-deadline meters (e.g., `T-04:12:49`).
  - DOI citations, ArXiv IDs, token metrics, and semantic search similarity indices.
  - Keyboard command accelerators (`⌘K`, `⇧P`).
- **Tabular Figures:** Always enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` across quantitative data displays, citation counts, and computational benchmark indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an IDE: dense, deterministic, and modular. It prioritizes maximizing operational density without inducing cognitive overload.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fluid 12-column or 16-column utility workbench anchored by persistent collapsible split-panes. Gutters hold strictly at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer margin paddings set to `2rem`.
- **Tablet / Split View (768px – 1279px):** Adaptive 8-column layout. Supplementary tool drawers collapse to contextual floating sheets or command bar actions. Gutters adjust to `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Sidebars convert to an instant-access bottom HUD drawer with `1rem` canvas safety margins.

### Spacing Principles
- **Dense Interior Padding:** Cards, badges, and list entries use deliberate, compact padding (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to ensure critical project metadata stays above the fold.
- **Macro Separation:** Section boundaries, workspace splits, and contextual view panels lean on structural dividers and `space-xl` gaps to maintain breathing room between disparate academic domains.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry drop shadows, muddy vignettes, and high-opacity diffusion. Spatial awareness is communicated entirely through tonal luminosity and precision boundaries.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Root Canvas `#090A0F`):** Flat workspace backing.
2. **Level 1 (Docked Containers `#0F1117`):** Flanked by a `1px` continuous border of `#232735`. Zero shadow.
3. **Level 2 (Active Panels & Cards `#161922`):** Enclosed by `#2E3446` with an optional micro top-edge highlight (`1px inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
4. **Level 3 (Overlays & Command HUDs `#1F2430`):** Floating dialogs receive a razor border (`#3B82F6` or `#2E3446`) with an ultra-tight, directional ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Precision Edge Highlighting
To impart a tactile, machined instrument aesthetic, high-priority interactive components implement an ultra-subtle directional rim light via CSS box-shadow inset:
`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08);`
This creates a crisp upper ridge reminiscent of milled hardware.

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, compact, and geometric. It defaults to a soft, razor-tuned curvature (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements crisp and technical while eliminating visual fatigue from harsh right angles.

### Shape Scale
- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-badges, code snippets, tags, input fields, and standard buttons.
- **Medium Elements (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Used for dropdown panels, nested card groups, and split-view tabs.
- **Containers (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for top-level workspace cards, terminal panes, and modal sheets.
- **Pill Exception (`9999px`):** Strictly used for runtime status indicators (e.g., active AI compile dot) and deadline timer chips.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric / Indigo):** Background `#6366F1` or `#00F59B` (for submission actions). Text is high-contrast obsidian (`#090A0F`) on lime, or `#FFFFFF` on indigo. Subtle inset upper rim highlight. `4px` radius, `0.5rem 0.875rem` padding.
- **Secondary (Tonal Wireframe):** Background `#161922`, border `1px solid #2E3446`, text `#F3F4F6`. Hover triggers background `#1F2430` and border `#3B82F6`.
- **Ghost Utility:** Borderless, text `#94A3B8`, hover text `#F3F4F6` with subtle `#161922` fill.

### 2. Badges & Telemetry Chips
- Rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`10px` or `11px`).
- Background: 10% opacity tinted fill matching the semantic token (e.g., `rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.08)` for Lime).
- Border: `1px solid` with 25% opacity matching tone.
- Text: Full saturation signal color. Include an optional leading pulsing dot (`6px`) for live processes.

### 3. Input Fields & Command Palettes
- Background `#0F1117`, border `1px solid #232735`.
- Text `#F3F4F6`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focus state: Border shifts instantly to `#3B82F6` or `#00F59B` with zero diffuse glow rings—just a clean, sharp `1px` transition.
- Embedded trailing keyboard shortcut badges (`label-mono-xs`) styled with `#161922` fill and `#2E3446` border.

### 4. Cards & Research Containers
- Background `#161922`, border `1px solid #232735`.
- Header bars within cards are separated by a hairline divider (`1px solid #232735`) featuring metadata on the right edge in monospace font.
- Never use elevated drop shadows for hover; indicate selection using border color shift to `#2E3446` or `#3B82F6`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Switches
- Checkboxes: Square `14px` x `14px`, `2px` corner radius, `1px solid #2E3446`. Checked state: `#00F59B` fill with `#090A0F` checkmark.
- Switches: Flat slider track (`#1F2430`), border `#2E3446`, handle `#94A3B8`. Active track switches to `#6366F1` with white thumb handle.

### 6. Precision Progress Meters & Urgency Bars
- Track height: `3px` or `4px`, background `#161922` with `1px` subtle track border.
- Indicator: Solid high-saturation accent.
  - Normal Pace: `#3B82F6`
  - Nearing Deadline: `#F59E0B`
  - Critical (<24h): `#EF4444` with subtle CSS linear transition.
  - Synced / Perfect: `#00F59B`
